FAQ

Commonquestions

Have a specific question? Message us directly.

How do I get a quote?

Fill in the form on our contact page, or WhatsApp us with the project type, date, location and what you want to achieve. We'll reply with a clear breakdown and quote.

Do you travel outside Kota Kinabalu, Sabah?

Yes. We shoot all over Sabah. Outstation jobs add a RM 400 travel and accommodation fee.

How fast is the turnaround?

It depends on the edit, but we agree a delivery date before shoot day so you can plan around it. Finished videos are delivered within 2 weeks or less.

Can we book video, photo and drone together?

Yes, and we recommend it. Shooting all three on one day saves time and budget and keeps the look consistent.

What formats will we receive?

16:9 for web and events, 9:16 vertical cuts for Instagram and TikTok, and high-resolution stills for print and digital.

How does payment work?

We take a 50% deposit to confirm the booking, and the balance is paid after the event or video is completed.
