Camera

The process

How aproject runs

  1. 01

    Brief

    Send the goal, date and location. We reply with a plan and a fixed quote.

  2. 02

    Pre-production

    Shot list, schedule, location check and drone flight plan, sorted before the shoot day.

  3. 03

    Shoot

    We arrive early, rig, shoot ground and air, and stay out of your guests' way.

  4. 04

    Edit & delivery

    Edit, colour and sound. One free round of changes, final files via Google Drive.
